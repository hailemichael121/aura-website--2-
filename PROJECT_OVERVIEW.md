# PROJECT OVERVIEW — AURA Website

## 1) Organization Overview
AURA (Alliance for Understanding Recovery & Awareness) is a mental health awareness and support organization based in Bole, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. The organization focuses on reducing stigma, supporting recovery journeys, and connecting people to trusted help.

## 2) Mission and Vision
### Mission
To normalize mental health conversations and build accessible, community-based support pathways across Ethiopian communities.

### Vision
A society where mental wellness support is treated as a right, and seeking help is met with dignity, understanding, and care.

## 3) Purpose of the Website
The website serves as AURA’s digital community hub by:
- Helping people quickly find immediate and non-emergency support.
- Explaining AURA’s mission, values, and programs.
- Encouraging safe community participation.
- Supporting partnerships, volunteering, and outreach.

## 4) Target Audience
- Young people and students navigating stress, anxiety, and life transitions.
- Adults and families seeking guidance and emotional support.
- Community members who want to participate in mental wellness conversations.
- Volunteers, schools, organizations, and health partners.

## 5) Design Philosophy
The redesigned website follows a calm, human-centered nonprofit style:
- Warm minimalism with deep green, white, and black text.
- Ethiopian/African contextual tone and imagery.
- Accessible and readable typography with generous spacing.
- Subtle motion and transitions, avoiding flashy effects.
- Supportive language focused on listening, understanding, and healing.

## 6) Technology Stack
- **Framework:** Next.js (App Router)
- **Language:** TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS
- **UI Utilities:** Radix-based components and shared UI primitives
- **Icons:** lucide-react

## 7) Core Information Architecture
Main navigation and page priorities:
- Home
- About
- Get Help
- Community
- Programs
- Contact
- Get Involved

## 8) Future Features
Potential roadmap enhancements:
1. Community forum moderation improvements and safety tooling.
2. Anonymous peer chat with escalation pathways.
3. Mental wellness resource library with Amharic and English content.
4. Event announcements and registration calendar.
5. Support group scheduling and attendance management.
6. Referral tracking dashboard for partner clinics and counselors.

## 9) Content and UX Principles
- Use plain, compassionate language instead of corporate jargon.
- Keep critical support actions visible and easy to access.
- Prioritize emotional safety, consent, and privacy.
- Maintain clarity for first-time visitors in distress.

## 10) Success Criteria
A successful AURA web experience should feel:
- Trustworthy
- Calm
- Community-oriented
- Culturally grounded
- Actionable for people seeking help
